# Official Logo & Branding Policy

This document clarifies the trademark and branding policy for this project.

The source code is freely available under the **Apache License, Version 2.0**.

---

## 🎨 Official Logo Protection

- **The trademark and copyright protection applies strictly to the official logo artwork** (the custom dial/rays icon and visual brand assets created by **Mortada (Dada)**).
- The name **"LineCounter" is NOT trademarked**. It is a generic descriptive term and is completely free for anyone to use without restriction.

---

## ✅ What Is Allowed

1. **Freedom of Name**:
   - Anyone is free to use the name "LineCounter" for forks, derivative tools, packages, or scripts.
2. **Code Modification & Redistribution**:
   - Fork, modify, enhance, package, and redistribute the software under Apache 2.0.
3. **Commercial Use**:
   - Incorporate the software into commercial products, cloud services, and proprietary distributions.
4. **Private Modifications**:
   - Modify privately for personal or internal enterprise use without publishing changes.

---

## 🚫 What Is NOT Allowed

1. **Official Logo Usage**:
   - Unofficial forks and derivative distributions may **not** use the official dial logo artwork as their application icon. Please supply your own logo.
2. **False Endorsement / Impersonation**:
   - You may not claim your fork or build is the official project, or that it is endorsed or maintained by Mortada (Dada).
3. **Removal of Attribution**:
   - Original copyright notices, the Apache 2.0 `LICENSE`, and author attribution to **Mortada (Dada)** in the `NOTICE` file must be preserved.
