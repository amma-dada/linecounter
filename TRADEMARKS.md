# LineCounter Trademark & Branding Policy

This policy outlines the rules regarding branding, project identity, and logo usage. While the underlying source code is freely available under the **Apache License, Version 2.0**, this document clarifies the brand and naming policy.

---

## 🏷️ The "LineCounter" Name (Generic & Free to Use)

- **The name "LineCounter" is descriptive and generic.**
- **Anyone is free to use the name "LineCounter"** for their forks, packages, scripts, or derivative tools. No trademark claim is asserted over the generic descriptive term "LineCounter" itself.
- You are welcome to name your derivative product "LineCounter" or any variation thereof.

---

## ✅ What You Are Allowed to Do

Under the Apache 2.0 License and this policy, you are free to:

1. **Use the Name "LineCounter"**:
   - Freely name your fork, derived tool, or package "LineCounter" or any other name you choose.
2. **Fork, Modify, and Redistribute**:
   - Freely fork the codebase, add features, fix bugs, optimize performance, and redistribute your modified code.
3. **Commercial Use & Selling**:
   - Incorporate the software into commercial products, cloud platforms, developer toolchains, or proprietary distributions for profit.
4. **Private Modifications**:
   - Make private changes for your organization, business, or personal workflow without any requirement to release your modifications.
5. **Create Competing Products**:
   - Build and release competing products or alternative tools derived from this codebase.

## 🚫 What Is NOT Allowed

To prevent deception and protect user trust:

1. **Do NOT Claim to Be the Original Creator**:
   - You may not claim authorship or invention of the original project. Original copyright notices and attribution to **Mortada (Dada)** must be preserved in all source distributions and NOTICE files.
2. **Do NOT Pretend to Be the Official Project**:
   - You may not state or imply that your fork, build, or service is the "official" LineCounter project, or that it is sponsored, maintained, or endorsed by Mortada (Dada).
3. **Do NOT Use the Official Original Logo for Unofficial Builds**:
   - Derivative works and unofficial builds must use their own application icon and should not use the official custom dial/rays artwork as their own branding.
4. **Do NOT Remove Required Copyright and License Notices**:
   - You must retain all copyright headers, the `LICENSE` (Apache 2.0), and the `NOTICE` file in all distributions and derivative works.
